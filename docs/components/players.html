Players

<o-video> and <o-audio> are custom controls built on the native <video>/<audio> elements: seek bar with buffered ranges, chapter markers and hover thumbnails, captions and speed menus, picture-in-picture, playlists, and a Web-Audio waveform for audio. data-o-video-preview adds hover-to-play cards.

Video, captions & chapters

Two caption tracks, three chapter markers on the seek bar, and scrubbing thumbnails (hover or drag the seek bar). Press C to cycle captions.

Playlist

playlist swaps src / poster / tracks and shows previous/next buttons; J / L seek ±10s, loop wraps at the ends.

Methods & events

Audio with a waveform

Peaks are computed once with the Web Audio API (decodeAudioData) and drawn on a canvas; click or drag to seek. Falls back to a plain progress bar when the source can't be decoded (CORS, format) or waveform is off.

Audio playlist & download

Hover-to-play cards (data-o-video-preview)

Hover a card (or focus it with the keyboard) — after a short delay a muted, looping preview clip fades in over the poster. Stays on the poster under prefers-reduced-motion.

Controls auto-hide a moment after the pointer stops moving during playback, and reappear on any interaction. The seek bar and volume are real sliders (role="slider" / a native <input type="range">) with a live aria-valuetext.
