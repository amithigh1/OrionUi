Phone input

<o-phone> pairs a searchable country selector with a national-format text field. It formats as you type, detects the country from a pasted +/00 international number, and always exposes (and submits) a normalised E.164 value.

Basic

Preferred countries & recent

preferred pins countries to the top of the list (e.g. the markets your product serves); the countries the visitor picked most recently are remembered too (recent, on by default, persisted in localStorage). Open the selector and search by name, ISO code or dial code.

Display format

format controls what is shown while the field is not being actively typed into (the value is always E.164): national (default), international, or e164.
