Form controls

Styles for native form elements. Everything stays a real input, so it works with any backend, framework or form library. Advanced controls (select, date picker, masks, OTP…) are covered in the other Forms pages.

Floating labels

Put the label after the control and give the control placeholder=" ".

Validation states

Put .is-invalid or .is-valid on the control or on .o-field, and messages in .o-error / .o-valid-msg appear. Add .o-validate to a form to style native :user-invalid fields automatically. See Form validation for the full validation engine.
