Heatmap, treemap & funnel

Grids and hierarchies: a sequential or diverging heatmap, a GitHub-style calendar, a squarified treemap and a conversion funnel. Magnitude uses one hue from light to dark — and the ramp is re-anchored for dark mode, where "near zero" recedes into the dark surface.

Heatmap

Rows are series, columns are labels (or pass flat { x, y, value } cells). Values are binned into up to seven steps of the sequential ramp, cells keep a 2px surface gap, and each cell has its own tooltip. A scale legend replaces the series legend.

Diverging heatmap

For values around a meaningful midpoint (change vs. last year, correlation, budget variance) set heatmap.scale: 'diverging': two opposite hues with a neutral gray at heatmap.mid (default 0). Values with labels that fit are printed in the cells in white or ink, chosen by the fill's luminance.

Calendar heatmap

GitHub-style contribution grid: weeks as columns, weekdays as rows (first day of week from the locale), month labels on top, and a "Less → More" legend. Days without activity use the neutral surface step.

Treemap

Squarified layout. Top-level groups take the categorical slots in order (and appear in the legend); their children share the group's hue and are separated by 2px surface gaps. Labels are drawn only inside tiles they fit.

Conversion funnel

Stages are ordered, so they take a one-hue ordinal ramp (darkest first) rather than categorical colors. The right column shows the share of the first stage and the step conversion from the previous one; below 460px the layout stacks the labels over the bars.

Options

OptionTypeDescription
heatmap.scale'sequential' 'diverging'One hue light→dark, or two poles with a neutral midpoint.
heatmap.bins3–7Number of color steps (default 7).
heatmap.min / max / midnumberFix the color domain (and diverging midpoint).
series[i].data[j] | data: [{ x, y, value }]Matrix rows, or flat cells (heatmap.yLabels orders rows).
calendar.from / toDate | stringVisible range (default: the 365 days ending at the last data point).
calendar.levels / max / weekStartnumberColor levels (4), value mapped to the top level, first weekday (from locale).
treemap data[{ name, value children }]One level of grouping; flat lists use a single hue.
funnellabels + one seriesStages in order; up to five use the validated ordinal ramp.
dataLabels'auto' true falseHeatmap cell values (auto when cells are at least 34px wide).

Keyboard: arrow keys move cell by cell (calendar: day/week), Home/End jump to the first/last cell.
