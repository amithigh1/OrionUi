Pie, donut & radial

Part-to-whole and multi-axis comparisons: pie, donut (with a center figure), radar, polar area and radial bars. Slices are separated by a 2px surface gap, labels are placed outside with leader lines when there is room, and small slices fold into "Other" rather than getting a ninth color.

When to use them

Pies and donuts answer "roughly what share?" at a glance — keep them to six or fewer segments and use a bar chart when values are close. Radar compares a few profiles across several axes; radial bars show progress of a handful of items against the same maximum.

Donut with center label

The center shows the total by default; hovering or focusing a slice shows that slice instead. Override it with pie.center: { value, label }.

Pie

Pass labels plus one series, or data: [{ label, value }]. More than eight slices fold the tail into "Other" (disable with fold: false). Legend buttons hide slices; the rest re-flow.

Radar

Each series is a polygon with a 10% wash; hovering snaps to the nearest axis and lists every series. Set radar.max for a fixed scale and radar.grid: 'circle' for round rings.

Polar area

Equal angles, radius proportional to the square root of the value so that slice area encodes magnitude honestly.

Radial bar

Concentric rings on a 270° sweep; each ring's track is a lighter step of its own hue, and labels sit in the open quadrant. radialBar.max defaults to 100.
