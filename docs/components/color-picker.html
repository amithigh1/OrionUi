Color picker

<o-colorpicker> is a form-associated color input: a saturation/brightness area, hue and alpha sliders, switchable hex/RGB/HSL fields, swatches, a persisted recent-colors list, an EyeDropper button where supported, and a quick WCAG contrast check against white and black.

Basic (popup)

Alpha channel & format

alpha adds the alpha slider and RGBA/HSLA output. format controls the serialized value/form value: hex (default), rgb or hsl — independent of which fields the user chooses to edit with in the panel.

Swatches & recent

swatches pins a curated palette at the top of the panel. Picked colors are also remembered in localStorage (recent, on by default) and shown in their own row.

Inline

inline renders the full panel in place (no trigger, no popup) — useful in a settings page or a dedicated theme editor.

EyeDropper

When the browser supports the EyeDropper API, a pipette button appears in the panel to sample any pixel on the screen. It is omitted automatically in unsupported browsers — no need to feature-detect yourself.

Contrast hint

Two chips under the fields show the WCAG contrast ratio of the current color against white and black (Orion.color.contrast), with a pass/fail mark at the 4.5:1 (AA, normal text) threshold. Disable with contrast-hint="false".

Keyboard

ControlKeysAction

Hue slider← → (Shift ×10), Home / EndAdjust hue 0-360.

The saturation/hue/alpha canvases are intentionally not mirrored in RTL — colors have no reading direction, the same choice native OS and browser color pickers make. Everything else (panel placement, field and button layout) is fully RTL-aware.
